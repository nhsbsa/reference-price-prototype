Reference Pricing Guidance

Originator identification

An originator presentation is the presentation used as the basis for establishing certain reference pricing information within a Virtual Therapeutic Moiety (VTM).

Originator information is used as part of the reference pricing methodology and may be displayed throughout the portal.

How are originator presentations identified?

Originator presentations are identified using available product and reference pricing information.

Where sufficient information is available, an originator presentation can be established and associated with a VTM.

When can an originator not be identified?

In some cases, an originator presentation cannot be identified from the available information.

This may occur where:

  • supporting information is unavailable
  • historical information cannot be confirmed
  • product relationships cannot be established
  • information is awaiting review or confirmation

When an originator presentation cannot be identified, it may be shown as Unknown.

What happens when an originator is unknown?

Some reference pricing activities can continue even when an originator presentation has not been identified.

Alternative approaches may be applied in accordance with the reference pricing methodology.

An unknown originator does not necessarily indicate that information is missing or incorrect.

Related information

You may also see:

  • Unknown originator presentation
  • Unknown Reference Anchor Date (RAD)
  • Products awaiting classification

These values do not necessarily indicate an error. They may reflect situations where information is unavailable, under review, or awaiting confirmation.
