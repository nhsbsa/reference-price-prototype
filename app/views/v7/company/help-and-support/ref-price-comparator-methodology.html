Reference Pricing Guidance

Comparator methodology

Comparator methodology is used as part of the reference pricing process to identify relevant products and presentations for comparison.

Comparator information helps support the establishment and maintenance of reference pricing information.

What is a comparator?

A comparator is a product or presentation that may be considered when establishing reference pricing information.

Comparators may be used to support decisions relating to reference pricing methodology.

How are comparators used?

Comparator information helps to:

  • identify relevant products within a VTM
  • support reference pricing activities
  • provide additional context for reference pricing information

Can comparator information change?

Yes.

Comparator information may change when:

  • new information becomes available
  • existing information is reviewed
  • product information changes
  • portfolio information is updated

Any applicable changes will be reflected within the portal.

Things to remember

  • Comparator information forms part of the wider reference pricing methodology.
  • Comparator information may be updated over time.
  • Not every product will have the same comparator information.
