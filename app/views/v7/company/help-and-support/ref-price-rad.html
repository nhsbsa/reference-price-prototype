Reference Pricing Guidance

Reference Anchor Dates (RADs)

A Reference Anchor Date (RAD) is a date used within the reference pricing methodology.

RAD information may be displayed alongside products, VTMs and other reference pricing information within the portal.

Why is a RAD important?

RADs provide information used within the reference pricing process and may help users understand how reference pricing information has been established.

You may see a RAD when viewing:

  • Product details
  • VTM information
  • Reference pricing information

Where can I find a RAD?

Where available, RAD information will be displayed within the relevant product or VTM record.

The date shown reflects the current information held within the portal.

Why might a RAD be unavailable?

A RAD may not always be available.

This may occur where:

  • supporting information is unavailable
  • an originator presentation has not been identified
  • information is under review
  • information is awaiting confirmation

Where a RAD cannot be established, it may be displayed as Unknown.

Can a RAD change?

Yes.

RAD information may be updated when additional information becomes available or when existing information is reviewed.

If a RAD changes, the updated value will be reflected within the portal.

Things to remember

  • Not every product or VTM will have an available RAD.
  • An unknown RAD does not necessarily indicate an error.
  • RAD information may be updated over time as information is reviewed and confirmed.
