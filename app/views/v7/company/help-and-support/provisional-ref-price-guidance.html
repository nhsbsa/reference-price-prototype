Provisional Reference Pricing Guidance

In some circumstances, reference pricing information may be published as provisional.

Providing provisional information allows users to access reference pricing data while supporting information is still being reviewed or confirmed.

What is provisional reference pricing?

Provisional reference pricing refers to reference pricing information that has not yet completed the full review and validation process.

The information is made available to improve visibility and transparency while review activities continue.

Why might information be provisional?

Information may be provisional where:

  • Supporting information is still being reviewed
  • Originator information is awaiting confirmation
  • Reference Anchor Date (RAD) information is under review
  • Product information has recently changed
  • Additional validation activity is required

What information might be provisional?

The provisional status may apply to:

  • Reference prices
  • Product classifications
  • Originator information
  • Reference Anchor Dates (RADs)
  • Other supporting reference pricing data

What happens after review?

Once review activities are complete, provisional information may:

  • Remain unchanged
  • Be confirmed and published as final reference pricing information
  • Be updated with new information
  • Be removed if it is no longer relevant or accurate

Any updates will be reflected within the portal.

Identifying provisional information

Where relevant, the portal will indicate when reference pricing information is provisional.

Users should always refer to the most recently published information available within the portal.

Things to remember

  • Provisional information is not necessarily incorrect..
  • A provisional status does not indicate an error.
  • Information may change as additional information becomes available.
  • Provisional information is provided to improve transparency while validation activities are ongoing.
