Reference Pricing Guidance

What is an originator?

An originator is the presentation identified as the original product within a Virtual Therapeutic Moiety (VTM).

Originator information may be used within the reference pricing methodology to help establish reference pricing information and Reference Anchor Dates (RADs).

Why is originator information important?

Originator information helps provide context for:

  • Reference pricing information
  • Reference Anchor Dates (RADs)
  • VTM relationships
  • Reference pricing methodology

You may see originator information when viewing products, VTMs and other reference pricing data within the portal.

Originator presentations

An originator presentation is the specific presentation identified as the originator within a VTM.

For example, a VTM may contain multiple products and presentations, but only one presentation may be identified as the originator.

When might an originator be unavailable?

In some cases, an originator presentation cannot be identified from the available information.

When this occurs:

  • the originator may be shown as Unknown
  • additional review may be required
  • reference pricing methodology may use alternative approaches

An unknown originator does not necessarily indicate an error.

Things to remember

  • Not all VTMs have an identifiable originator presentation.
  • Originator information may change if new information becomes available.
  • Originator information is used as part of the wider reference pricing methodology.
