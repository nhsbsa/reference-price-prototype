VPAG Guidance

The Voluntary Scheme for Branded Medicines Pricing, Access and Growth (VPAG) is an agreement between the Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC), NHS England and participating pharmaceutical companies.

Reference pricing forms part of the wider VPAG framework and supports the management of medicines within the scheme.

How does VPAG relate to the portal?

The portal provides access to information that supports reference pricing activities, including:

  • Reference prices
  • Reference Anchor Dates (RADs)
  • Originator information
  • Product classifications
  • Historical reference pricing information

This information is provided to improve transparency and help companies understand reference pricing data associated with their products.

Understanding reference pricing information

Information displayed within the portal may change over time as information is reviewed, updated or confirmed.

You may see information that is:

  • Confirmed
  • Provisional
  • Unknown
  • Awaiting classification

These values do not necessarily indicate an error.

Things to remember

  • VPAG provides the framework within which reference pricing operates.
  • Information may be reviewed and updated over time.
  • Historical information may remain available for reference purposes.
  • Some information may be unavailable while validation or review activities are ongoing.
