Department of Health and Social Care Medicines Reporting User Agreement (MRUA)

This user agreement is a legal agreement between you (either an individual or a single entity) and the Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC).

DHSC grants you the right to access DHSC’s online system, the Department of Health Medicines Reporting provided that you comply with all the terms and conditions of this MRUA.

The service comprises:

  • Branded Medicines
  • Price Concessions
  • Quarterly Collections

The Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service includes:

  • computer software
  • the data supplied with it
  • electronic documentation and internet-based services

The right of access cannot be transferred to anyone else.

Definitions

Confidential information means any information containing personal data, financial information and any other information clearly designated as being confidential (whether it is marked as 'confidential' or not) or which would reasonably be considered confidential.

Data breach means a breach of security or security incident which leads to the accidental, wrongful or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to data or information contained within or obtained via the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service.

Personal data means information relating to natural persons who can be identified or who are identifiable directly from the information or who can be indirectly identified from the information in combination with other information.

User obligations

The Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal is provided to facilitate the submission, management and review of medicines-related information required by the Department of Health and Social Care.

This includes:

  • Branded Medicines submissions, including the calculation of eligible sales for companies participating in the voluntary and statutory schemes
  • Price Concessions submissions and supporting information
  • Quarterly Collections submissions
  • product pricing information
  • stock level information
  • purchase and sales information
  • product and scheme administration data

The Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal may not be used:

  • for personal purposes
  • to access any information via the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal unless necessary for the performance of your duties for the NHS and/or wider Government commissioned services

You are responsible for providing accurate information and ensuring the accurate production of any reports produced through the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service. DHSC shall not be liable for any losses or damage incurred by inappropriate or inaccurate use of any data provided through the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal or reliance upon any inaccurate reports produced by users.

You agree not to publish or to use or include in any material which will be made public, any information obtained via Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal without the prior written consent of DHSC.

Access to the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal through any allocated user ID is restricted to one named registered user. You must not share access, passwords, usernames or log on details to the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal with any other person. Passwords must not be written down.

You must not access the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal:

  • when you no longer require the use of the system
  • when your role or circumstances change such that you no longer satisfy the terms of this agreement
  • using an email address/account which you no longer use, or which has become inactive

You must notify DHSC of the above as soon as possible so that DHSC records can be updated and the account can be withdrawn or amended as necessary.

You must not access the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service from outside the UK. The DHSC reserves the right to restrict access from any non-UK IP addresses.

You agree that DHSC has the right to:

  • audit your and/or your employer’s use of the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal and you and/or your employer will provide information and reasonable cooperation to DHSC upon request
  • withdraw access to Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal where you have breached or no longer satisfy the terms of this agreement, where any other abuse of the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal is understood to have occurred (including unreasonably excessive use) or there has been a data breach
  • suspend an individual’s access to Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal pending an DHSC investigation into a user’s breach of the MRUA, other abuse of the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service or a data breach, where DHSC considers it appropriate to do so

You are responsible for informing DHSC if you:

  • have access to information you do not consider to be appropriate to your role
  • become aware of a data breach which does not concern any personal data
  • consider your organisation's data and security standards, policies and procedures and technical and organisational measures are not equivalent to the standard of security required by NHS security policies

You should report inappropriate access and data breaches to DHSC via email at DataServicesSupport@nhsbsa.nhs.uk as soon as possible and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware.

Contact DHSC via email at DataServicesSupport@nhsbsa.nhs.uk if you require information on the data and security standards that are required by DHSC to be in place. DHSC reserves the right to audit your organisation's measures, standards, policies and procedures.

Freedom of information requests

For the purposes of Freedom of Information (FOI) legislation, Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal information is held by the DHSC. You agree not to use the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal to download information to answer an FOI request.  You must refer the FOI requester to mailto:foirequests@nhsbsa.nhs.uk.

The only exception to this is where you have downloaded Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal information to your local systems before the FOI request was received.  Where this is the case, you agree to consult with DHSC at DataServicesSupport@nhsbsa.nhs.uk before responding to the FOI request.

Subject access requests

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you. View DHSC’s Privacy Policy for information on how to make a subject access request.

Personal data

You agree that you will:

  • comply with all data and security standards, policies and procedures applicable to you as an employee or contractor of the NHS or wider Government commissioned service
  • only use, hold and distribute data accessed via the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal within the NHS/wider Government and solely as required for the purposes of your employment
  • destroy data in a secure manner in accordance with any applicable legislation and/or NHS data retention policies and/or the instruction of DHSC, where data you have obtained via the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal is no longer required

You agree that you and your employer have in place appropriate technical and organisational measures to prevent unauthorised or unlawful processing of personal data and to prevent accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to personal data which are at least equivalent to the standard of security required by the NHS security policies, and you and your employer shall take reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of any employees who will have access to Personal Data.

You will be liable for any breach of confidence, breach of Data Protection legislation and/or data breach which involves any personal data and upon becoming aware of such an incident you may be required to report this to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as soon as possible.

DHSC must also be advised of any data breach or suspected data breach concerning personal data without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours of you becoming aware of such a breach or suspected breach, via email at DataServicesSupport@nhsbsa.nhs.uk.

Any small number, rate or percentage derived from DHSC data must be suppressed if there is a risk of identification. Figures that may identify individuals when subtracted from totals, subtotals or other published figures must also be suppressed.

Confidentiality

You acknowledge that in using the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal you may have access to confidential information with respect to medicines pricing information, commercial sales data, scheme payment information, product information, purchase and sales information, stock and supply information, organisation-specific submissions and reports and other commercially sensitive information submitted through the Service.

You agree that you shall hold any confidential information in confidence and, unless required by law, shall not:

  • make any confidential information available to any third party (other than to a party to whom you are satisfied that you may legally disclose such confidential information)
  • use the confidential information for any purpose otherwise than in the proper performance of your duties as an NHS employee or contractor (as applicable)
  • permit or cause any unauthorised disclosure of confidential information through any failure to exercise due care and diligence
  • release any data in public that could allow information about an individual that is not already public to become identifiable, or able to be deduced from any other data

Cookies

You agree that cookies are enabled on the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal. The purpose of the cookie is to aid the login process by ensuring your user details do not have to be re-entered.

The information relating to cookies can be found within the applicable privacy and cookie policies published by DHSC and/or NHS Business Services Authority.

Login details

All the information you provide when you register will be used to manage access to the Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal.

The email address you provide may also be used by DHSC for service-related communications and sending you newsletters relevant to our Information Systems.

If required, we may use the email address provided to:

  • contact you to complete surveys relating to DHSC services and systems
  • invite you to take part in research to help improve our services to you

If you need to update, receive a copy of or delete your registration information then please contact DataServicesSupport@nhsbsa.nhs.uk.

Audits will be carried out every 6 months by DHSC on Department of Health Medicines Reporting Service portal user accounts to review access requirements.

We will keep your information for 12 months after your account is closed.  This is to ensure that all transactions can be fully audited.

We do not disclose this information to third party organisations.
