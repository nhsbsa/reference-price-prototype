Branded Medicines

Introduction

This portal allows companies to submit their quarterly sales reports, annual audited sales reports, and the annual unaudited presentation level data, which supports a payment company's quarterly sales reports.

This portal also allows companies to provide supporting documentation required for submission to the Department under both the Voluntary Scheme and Statutory Scheme.


Contact

For any queries/clarification, please email your Branded Medicines Operations contact at the Department or alternatively email the Branded Medicines Mailbox dh.brandedmedicines@dhsc.gov.uk

For more information on the 2024 Voluntary Scheme, 2019 Voluntary Scheme and Statutory Scheme, visit the Branded Medicines Information Site (opens in new tab).
